# Competitor Profile

Build a comprehensive competitor profile for **$ARGUMENTS**.

## Instructions

Research and deliver a structured profile covering:

1. **Company Overview:** Headquarters, founded, ownership, employee count, mission
2. **Financial Snapshot:** Revenue, growth rate, margins, funding/market cap (what's publicly available)
3. **Products & Services:** Full portfolio, flagship offerings, pricing model
4. **Target Market:** Customer segments, verticals, geographic focus, company size focus
5. **Go-to-Market:** Sales model, marketing approach, partnerships, distribution channels
6. **Technology & IP:** Known tech stack, patents, R&D signals
7. **Recent Strategic Moves:** M&A, product launches, partnerships, key hires (last 12-24 months)
8. **Strengths (3-5):** Evidence-based, with specific examples
9. **Weaknesses (3-5):** Evidence-based, with specific examples
10. **Strategic Outlook:** What are they likely to do next? Based on signals.

Use web search for current data. Cite all sources. Flag confidence levels.

Format as a structured report. Open with a 3-sentence overview paragraph.
