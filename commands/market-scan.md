# Quick Market Scan

Conduct a rapid market scan for **$ARGUMENTS**.

## Instructions

Deliver a concise market overview (2-3 pages) covering:

1. **Market Size & Growth:** TAM estimate with source, 3-year CAGR, growth outlook
2. **Key Players:** Top 5-7 competitors with estimated market share
3. **Industry Structure:** Porter's Five Forces summary (rate each H/M/L in a table)
4. **Key Trends:** Top 3-5 trends shaping the industry (include trend direction and timeframe)
5. **Regulatory Environment:** Key regulations, recent changes, upcoming changes
6. **"So What" for the Client:** 3-5 strategic implications

Use web search to find current data. For every data point, cite the source and year. Flag data confidence as High/Medium/Low.

Format as a structured report with action-oriented headers. Start with a 3-bullet executive summary.
