## Usage Guide

### How to Select KPIs
1. **Start with strategy:** What are the organization's top 3-5 strategic priorities?
2. **Identify 2-3 KPIs per priority:** Both leading (predictive) and lagging (outcome) indicators
3. **Limit executive dashboards to 7-10 KPIs:** More than that creates noise
4. **Ensure each KPI has an owner:** Someone accountable for monitoring and improving it
5. **Set targets:** Aspirational but achievable, informed by benchmarks and historical trends

### Leading vs. Lagging Indicators
- **Lagging indicators** tell you what happened (revenue, profit, churn)
- **Leading indicators** predict what will happen (pipeline, NPS, engagement)
- Every dashboard should include both types for a complete view

### Benchmark Sources
- **SaaS:** KeyBanc SaaS Survey, OpenView Benchmarks, Bessemer Cloud Index
- **General Business:** APQC, Hackett Group, Gartner
- **HR:** SHRM, Mercer, Bureau of Labor Statistics
- **Finance:** CFO.com, Deloitte CFO Signals
- **Operations:** ISM, APICS, industry-specific associations
