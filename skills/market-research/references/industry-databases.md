A curated reference of data sources for market research and industry analysis, organized by access type and sector. For each source, key details are provided: what data it offers, approximate cost, and how frequently it is updated.

## Source Selection Guidance

When choosing data sources for a market research project, use this decision framework:

1. **Start with free public sources** to establish baseline facts (market size order of magnitude, growth direction, key players).
2. **Use paid reports** when you need precise segmentation, forecasts with methodology, or competitive market share data.
3. **Layer in alternative data** when you need leading indicators, competitive signals, or validation of assumptions.
4. **Always cite sources** with the year of publication. A 2022 market size from Statista is materially different from a 2025 estimate from Grand View Research.
5. **Cross-reference at least two sources** for any critical market size number. If two reputable sources disagree by more than 30%, investigate the methodological differences.
