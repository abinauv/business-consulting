# Retention Playbook

## Flight Risk Indicators

### 15 Leading Indicators

| # | Indicator | Data Source | Risk Signal | Lead Time |
|---|----------|-----------|------------|:---------:|
| 1 | **Resume updated on LinkedIn** | LinkedIn monitoring | Profile photo, headline, or summary changed | 30–60 days |
| 2 | **Engagement score decline** | Survey data | Dropped >1 point since last survey | 60–90 days |
| 3 | **Manager relationship** | 360/survey | Low score on "My manager cares about me" | 90+ days |
| 4 | **Passed over for promotion** | HRIS | Eligible but not promoted in last 2 cycles | 30–90 days |
| 5 | **Compensation below market** | Comp analysis | Compa-ratio <85% | Ongoing |
| 6 | **Tenure milestone** | HRIS | Approaching 2-year or 4-year (vesting) anniversary | 30–60 days |
| 7 | **Life event** | Manager knowledge | Relocation, marriage, new child, partner job change | Varies |
| 8 | **Team/org changes** | HRIS | New manager, reorg, peers leaving | 30–60 days |
| 9 | **Workload complaints** | Manager/surveys | Sustained overwork, burnout signals | 60–90 days |
| 10 | **PTO pattern change** | HRIS | Using PTO for single days (interview days) | 14–30 days |
| 11 | **Reduced participation** | Manager observation | Fewer contributions in meetings, withdrawn | 30–60 days |
| 12 | **Peer departures** | HRIS | 2+ close colleagues left recently | 30–90 days |
| 13 | **Skills mismatch** | Performance data | Overqualified for current role, stagnating | 90+ days |
| 14 | **Industry hot market** | External data | Their role is in high demand (wage inflation >10%) | Ongoing |
| 15 | **Loss of meaning** | Surveys/1:1s | "I don't see how my work matters" | 60–90 days |

### Flight Risk Scoring Model

| Factor | Weight | Low Risk (1) | Medium (3) | High Risk (5) |
|--------|:------:|-------------|-----------|--------------|
| Compensation vs. market | 25% | Above P60 | P40–P60 | Below P40 |
| Manager relationship | 20% | Strong (4+/5 on survey) | Neutral (3/5) | Poor (<3/5) |
| Career progression | 20% | Promoted in last 18 mo | On track, clear path | Stalled, no path visible |
| Engagement score | 15% | Above company avg | At average | Below average |
| Market demand for role | 10% | Low demand | Moderate | High demand, hot market |
| Tenure risk | 10% | Mid-tenure (1–3 years) | New (<6 mo) or approaching cliff | Vesting cliff in <3 mo |

**Score interpretation:**
- 1.0–2.0: Low risk (monitor quarterly)
- 2.1–3.5: Medium risk (proactive check-in, review comp)
- 3.6–5.0: High risk (immediate intervention)

## Stay Interview Guide

### When to Conduct
- High performers (proactively, annually)
- High flight-risk individuals (when indicators trigger)
- After a peer departure (within 2 weeks)
- After an organizational change (reorg, leadership change)

### 10 Stay Interview Questions

1. **"What do you look forward to when you come to work each day?"** (Identifies motivators)
2. **"What are you learning here? What do you want to learn?"** (Growth needs)
3. **"Why do you stay at [Company]?"** (Current retention drivers)
4. **"When was the last time you thought about leaving? What prompted it?"** (Risk triggers)
5. **"What would make your job more satisfying?"** (Improvement opportunities)
6. **"Do you feel your talents are being fully utilized?"** (Engagement and utilization)
7. **"What's one thing you would change about your role or team?"** (Pain points)
8. **"How do you feel about the recognition you receive?"** (Recognition needs)
9. **"Is there another role in the company you'd be interested in?"** (Internal mobility)
10. **"What can I do to make your experience here better?"** (Manager relationship)

### Post-Interview Action Plan

| What I Heard | Priority | Action I'll Take | By When | Status |
|-------------|:--------:|-----------------|---------|:------:|
| [Theme from interview] | [H/M/L] | [Specific action] | [Date] | [Open/Done] |

**Critical rule:** If you conduct a stay interview, you MUST act on at least one thing within 2 weeks. Asking and not acting is worse than not asking.

## Exit Interview Analysis Framework

### Coding Themes

| Theme | Sub-themes | Questions to Ask |
|-------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Compensation** | Base pay, bonus, equity, benefits | "Was compensation a factor? How so?" |
| **Career growth** | Promotion, development, learning | "Did you feel you had a clear growth path?" |
| **Manager** | Relationship, feedback, support, micromanagement | "How was your relationship with your manager?" |
| **Culture** | Values, inclusion, collaboration, politics | "How would you describe the culture?" |
| **Work** | Workload, autonomy, challenge, impact | "Were you engaged by your work?" |
| **Company direction** | Strategy, leadership confidence, stability | "How do you feel about where the company is headed?" |
| **Life/personal** | Relocation, family, health, retirement | "Were there personal factors in your decision?" |

### Analyzing Exit Data

1. **Code each exit** with primary and secondary reasons
2. **Track trends** quarterly (are certain themes increasing?)
3. **Segment by** department, manager, tenure, performance, demographics
4. **Calculate "regrettable" turnover** — high performers in critical roles who leave voluntarily
5. **Identify systemic issues** — if 3+ exits cite the same manager or the same concern, it's systemic

## Retention Metrics Dashboard

| Metric | Definition | Target | Frequency |
|--------|-----------|:------:|:---------:|
| **Overall voluntary turnover** | Voluntary separations ÷ Avg headcount | <15% (varies by industry) | Monthly |
| **Regrettable turnover** | High performer voluntary exits ÷ High performers | <8% | Monthly |
| **90-day turnover** | Exits within first 90 days ÷ New hires | <10% | Monthly |
| **Turnover by manager** | Voluntary exits per manager (normalize by team size) | Flag >2x company average | Quarterly |
| **Turnover by tenure** | Distribution of exits by tenure cohort | Identify spikes | Quarterly |
| **Retention rate** | Employees retained ÷ Starting headcount (cohort-based) | >85% annual | Monthly |
| **Average tenure** | Mean tenure of current employees | Industry benchmark | Quarterly |
| **eNPS** | % Promoters − % Detractors (employee Net Promoter Score) | >30 | Semi-annual |
| **Flight risk coverage** | % of high-risk employees with active retention plan | 100% | Monthly |
| **Offer acceptance rate** | Offers accepted ÷ Offers extended | >85% | Monthly |
| **Regrettable turnover cost** | Total cost of regrettable turnover (using calculator above) | Minimize YoY | Quarterly |
