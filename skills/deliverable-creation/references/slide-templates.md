# Consulting Slide Templates

Standard slide types used in management consulting. For each: purpose, layout, when to use, and example.

---

## 1. Title Slide
**Purpose:** Open the presentation with project name, client name, and date.
**Layout:** Centered title (large), subtitle with client name, date bottom-right, confidentiality notice bottom-left, firm logo if applicable.
**When to use:** First slide of every presentation.
**Example title:** "European Market Entry Strategy — Board Discussion"

## 2. Agenda Slide
**Purpose:** Orient the audience on the presentation structure.
**Layout:** Numbered list of 3-5 sections. Currently active section highlighted in color, others in gray. Optional time estimates per section.
**When to use:** After the title slide, and repeated before each new section (with current section highlighted).
**Example:** "1. Market Opportunity | 2. Entry Options | 3. Financial Analysis | 4. Implementation Plan"

## 3. Executive Summary
**Purpose:** Distill the entire presentation into one slide for time-pressed executives.
**Layout:** Action title at top. Body: 3-5 key finding bullets, each with **bolded lead-in phrase** followed by one sentence of supporting detail. Optional: recommendation callout box at bottom.
**When to use:** Immediately after agenda. Required on every consulting deck.
**Example title:** "We recommend acquiring EuroDistributor for $45M to enter Europe and achieve $100M revenue in 5 years"

## 4. Key Finding with Chart
**Purpose:** Present quantitative evidence supporting one insight.
**Layout:** Action title (states the insight, not the chart topic). Single chart taking 60-70% of slide area. 2-3 bullet annotations on the side or below. Source note bottom-left.
**When to use:** Whenever presenting data-driven evidence. Most common slide type.
**Example title:** "Enterprise segment drove 80% of revenue growth, offsetting SMB decline"

## 5. Comparison (Side-by-Side)
**Purpose:** Compare two options, scenarios, or time periods.
**Layout:** Action title. Two columns (50/50 split or 60/40). Each column has a header, key metrics, and supporting points. Use color to indicate the recommended option (blue for recommended, gray for the other).
**When to use:** Comparing two strategies, before/after states, or two competitors.
**Example title:** "Option A delivers higher returns but carries greater execution risk than Option B"

## 6. Harvey Ball Matrix
**Purpose:** Compare multiple options across multiple qualitative criteria.
**Layout:** Action title. Table with options as columns, criteria as rows. Cells contain Harvey balls: full circle (strong), 3/4 (good), 1/2 (moderate), 1/4 (weak), empty (poor). Overall score row at bottom. Recommended option column highlighted.
**When to use:** Comparing 3+ options across 5-8 qualitative dimensions.
**Example title:** "Option B scores highest across all criteria, particularly on strategic fit and feasibility"

## 7. Process Flow
**Purpose:** Show a sequence of steps, a value chain, or a workflow.
**Layout:** Action title. Horizontal chevrons or boxes connected by arrows (5-7 steps max). Each step has a label and optional 1-line description below. Color one or two steps to highlight focus areas.
**When to use:** Process improvements, implementation plans, customer journeys, value chains.
**Example title:** "The current order-to-cash process has 12 handoffs, with 3 bottlenecks causing 70% of delays"

## 8. Timeline / Roadmap
**Purpose:** Show a phased implementation plan with milestones.
**Layout:** Action title. Horizontal timeline with 3-4 phases (bands of color). Key milestones as diamonds on the timeline. Activities listed below each phase. Owners and duration noted.
**When to use:** Implementation plans, transformation programs, project plans.
**Example title:** "18-month integration plan in three phases: Close (Q1), Stabilize (Q2-Q3), and Grow (Q4+)"

## 9. SWOT 4-Quadrant
**Purpose:** Present SWOT analysis results.
**Layout:** Action title. 2x2 grid: Strengths (top-left, green), Weaknesses (top-right, red), Opportunities (bottom-left, blue), Threats (bottom-right, orange). 4-5 bullets per quadrant. Labels: "Internal" (top row), "External" (bottom row), "Positive" (left column), "Negative" (right column).
**When to use:** Situation assessment, competitive analysis.
**Example title:** "Strong brand and IP position the company well, but operational inefficiency and legacy tech create vulnerability"

## 10. 2x2 Matrix
**Purpose:** Position items along two strategic dimensions to reveal quadrant insights.
**Layout:** Action title. X-axis and Y-axis with clear labels and direction indicators (low to high). Four quadrants with descriptive labels. Items plotted as labeled circles (size can encode a third dimension). Quadrant interpretation in a small legend or annotation.
**When to use:** BCG matrix, impact vs. effort prioritization, strategic group mapping, risk assessment.
**Example title:** "Three initiatives are Quick Wins (high impact, low effort) — prioritize these for immediate action"

## 11. Waterfall / Bridge Chart
**Purpose:** Show how a metric builds up or changes from one state to another.
**Layout:** Action title. Horizontal bar chart with a starting bar (blue), incremental bars (green for positive, red for negative), and ending bar (blue). Value labels on each bar. Category labels below.
**When to use:** Revenue bridges (FY24 → FY25), cost breakdowns, margin walks, variance analysis.
**Example title:** "Revenue grew $5M driven by volume (+$3M) and pricing (+$4M), partially offset by churn (-$2M)"

## 12. Stacked Bar with Annotations
**Purpose:** Show composition and trends over time.
**Layout:** Action title. Vertical stacked bars (one per time period). Each segment in a distinct color with legend. Total value label on top of each bar. Key annotations (callout boxes with arrows) highlighting important changes.
**When to use:** Revenue by segment over time, cost composition changes, market share evolution.
**Example title:** "International revenue grew from 15% to 28% of total, driven by European expansion"

## 13. Text Slide (3 Bullets)
**Purpose:** Present qualitative arguments or recommendations.
**Layout:** Action title. Three bullets, each with:
- **Bolded lead-in phrase** (5-8 words) — states the point
- Supporting sentence (1-2 lines) — provides evidence or context
Maximum 3 bullets. If you need more, split into two slides.
**When to use:** Recommendations, qualitative findings, strategic implications.
**Example title:** "Three actions will close the operational gap and restore margins to 12%"

## 14. Case Study / Example
**Purpose:** Illustrate a point with a real-world example.
**Layout:** Action title. Left side: company logo, key facts (industry, size, challenge). Right side: what they did, results achieved (with quantified outcomes). Optional: quote or testimonial.
**When to use:** Benchmarking, best practice examples, proof-of-concept evidence.
**Example title:** "CompanyX achieved 30% cost reduction through the same shared services model we recommend"

## 15. Next Steps / Action Items
**Purpose:** Close the presentation with clear actions and accountability.
**Layout:** Action title. Table with columns: Action Item | Owner | Deadline | Status. 5-7 items maximum. Group by immediate (this week), near-term (this month), and medium-term (this quarter) if needed.
**When to use:** Last content slide of every presentation.
**Example title:** "Three decisions needed this week to maintain the project timeline"

## 16. Appendix Divider
**Purpose:** Separate main content from backup material.
**Layout:** Simple page with "Appendix" centered. Optional: table of contents listing appendix sections.
**When to use:** Before any backup slides.

## 17. Data Table
**Purpose:** Present detailed quantitative data for reference.
**Layout:** Action title. Clean table with alternating row shading. Column headers bolded. Key cells highlighted (bold, color). Source note. No vertical lines — use white space and horizontal lines only.
**When to use:** Financial summaries, detailed benchmarking data, scenario comparison, supporting data.
**Example title:** "Detailed peer comparison confirms our client's SGA costs are 8pp above median"

---

## Universal Formatting Rules

| Element | Standard |
|---------|----------|
| Title font | 18-20pt, bold, sentence case |
| Body font | 11-12pt, regular |
| Source note | 8pt, bottom-left, gray |
| Colors | Primary: dark blue (#2F5496). Accent: teal (#00B0F0). Positive: green (#70AD47). Negative: red (#FF5050). Neutral: gray (#A6A6A6) |
| Margins | 0.5 inch on all sides |
| Alignment | Left-aligned text, center-aligned charts |
| Page number | Bottom-right, 8pt |
| Confidentiality | Bottom-left: "Confidential — prepared for [Client Name]" |
