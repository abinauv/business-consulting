# Competitor Profiling Checklist

A comprehensive checklist of 100+ data points to collect for each competitor. Use this as a structured guide during research. Not every data point will be available for every competitor; prioritize based on strategic relevance and mark items as "Not Available" or "Not Applicable" when needed.

---

## 1. Company Overview (17 items)

- [ ] Legal entity name
- [ ] Doing business as (DBA) / trade names
- [ ] Headquarters location (city, state/province, country)
- [ ] Year founded
- [ ] Ownership type (public, private, PE-backed, VC-backed, family-owned, government-owned)
- [ ] Stock ticker and exchange (if public)
- [ ] Parent company (if subsidiary)
- [ ] Key subsidiaries and affiliates
- [ ] Total employee count (current)
- [ ] Employee count growth trend (last 3 years, percentage change year-over-year)
- [ ] Office locations (list all major offices and their functions: HQ, engineering, sales, support)
- [ ] Mission statement and/or vision statement
- [ ] Company culture indicators (Glassdoor rating, employee reviews themes, stated values)
- [ ] Key milestones in company history (founding, pivots, IPO, major product launches)
- [ ] Awards and industry recognition
- [ ] ESG initiatives and sustainability commitments
- [ ] Corporate structure and organizational design (functional, divisional, matrix)

## 2. Leadership & Governance (12 items)

- [ ] CEO name, background, tenure, and prior roles
- [ ] C-suite team (CFO, CTO, CMO, COO, CPO, CRO) with backgrounds
- [ ] Board of directors: names, affiliations, and relevant expertise
- [ ] Advisory board members (if disclosed)
- [ ] Key technical leaders (VP Engineering, Chief Architect, Head of AI/ML)
- [ ] Average leadership tenure (indicator of stability or turnover)
- [ ] Recent leadership changes (last 24 months) and context for departures
- [ ] Executive compensation data (public companies: proxy statement disclosures)
- [ ] Insider ownership percentage (public companies: alignment signal)
- [ ] Management style and decision-making culture (centralized vs. decentralized, data from interviews/articles)
- [ ] Founder involvement (still active, advisory role, departed)
- [ ] Key leadership hires in the last 12 months (signals strategic priorities)

## 3. Financial Performance (16 items)

- [ ] Total revenue (current fiscal year)
- [ ] Revenue for prior 3 years (to calculate growth trend)
- [ ] Revenue growth rate (year-over-year and 3-year CAGR)
- [ ] Revenue breakdown by segment or product line
- [ ] Revenue breakdown by geography
- [ ] Gross margin (current and trend)
- [ ] EBITDA margin (current and trend)
- [ ] Net profit margin (current and trend)
- [ ] Cash and cash equivalents on hand
- [ ] Total debt and debt-to-equity ratio
- [ ] Capital expenditure as a percentage of revenue
- [ ] R&D spending as a percentage of revenue
- [ ] Revenue per employee (efficiency metric)
- [ ] Free cash flow (current and trend)
- [ ] Funding rounds: dates, amounts, investors, and valuations (for private companies)
- [ ] Burn rate and estimated runway (for startups and pre-profit companies)

## 4. Products & Services (16 items)

- [ ] Complete product and service portfolio (list every offering)
- [ ] Flagship product(s) and their share of total revenue
- [ ] Product roadmap (if publicly shared, inferred from blog posts, conference talks, or job postings)
- [ ] Pricing model (subscription, usage-based, per-seat, one-time, freemium, hybrid)
- [ ] Published price points for each tier or SKU
- [ ] Free tier or freemium offering details (feature limitations, usage caps)
- [ ] Product quality indicators (uptime SLA, performance benchmarks, third-party reviews)
- [ ] Key product differentiators (unique features, proprietary technology, UX advantages)
- [ ] Underlying technology platform and architecture (cloud-native, on-premise, hybrid)
- [ ] Integration ecosystem (number and quality of integrations, marketplace, API availability)
- [ ] Release cadence (weekly, monthly, quarterly releases)
- [ ] Aggregate customer review scores (G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, App Store)
- [ ] Net Promoter Score (if publicly available or estimable)
- [ ] Product-market fit signals (retention metrics, usage growth, organic word-of-mouth)
- [ ] Product-led growth metrics (free-to-paid conversion, expansion revenue signals)
- [ ] End-of-life or deprecated products (indicates strategic shifts)

## 5. Market & Customers (16 items)

- [ ] Primary target customer segments (by industry, size, function)
- [ ] Ideal customer profile (ICP) as described by the competitor
- [ ] Customer concentration (percentage of revenue from top 10 customers, if estimable)
- [ ] Largest named customers (from case studies, press releases, SEC filings)
- [ ] Customer logos displayed on website (count and quality)
- [ ] Published case studies (number, industries, outcomes highlighted)
- [ ] Customer retention rate or churn rate (if disclosed or estimable)
- [ ] Net Promoter Score or customer satisfaction metrics
- [ ] Primary customer acquisition channels (inbound, outbound, referral, partner, PLG)
- [ ] Geographic markets served (countries, regions, expansion trajectory)
- [ ] Vertical or industry specialization (deep vs. horizontal)
- [ ] Typical contract structure (monthly, annual, multi-year, usage-based)
- [ ] Average contract value or deal size (if estimable)
- [ ] Customer lifetime value signals (contract length x deal size x retention)
- [ ] Published testimonials and reference customers
- [ ] Community engagement (user community size, forum activity, user group events)

## 6. Go-to-Market Strategy (12 items)

- [ ] Sales model (product-led growth, inside sales, field sales, channel/partner, hybrid)
- [ ] Estimated sales team size (headcount from LinkedIn, job postings)
- [ ] Marketing spend indicators (estimated budget, share of voice, ad volume)
- [ ] Brand awareness level (aided and unaided, survey data if available)
- [ ] Content marketing presence (blog frequency, quality, thought leadership)
- [ ] SEO strength (domain authority, organic keyword rankings, traffic estimates)
- [ ] Social media presence and engagement (followers, posting frequency, engagement rate by platform)
- [ ] Event and conference presence (sponsorships, keynotes, booth presence, hosted events)
- [ ] Strategic partnerships and alliances (technology, distribution, co-selling)
- [ ] Channel and reseller strategy (partner program, number of partners, partner tiers)
- [ ] Customer success and support model (dedicated CSM, self-serve, tiered support)
- [ ] Marketing messaging and positioning (tagline, value proposition, key claims)

## 7. Technology & Innovation (11 items)

- [ ] Core technology stack (languages, frameworks, infrastructure, cloud provider)
- [ ] Patent portfolio (number of patents granted, number pending, key patent areas)
- [ ] R&D headcount (absolute and as percentage of total employees)
- [ ] Open-source contributions (projects maintained, contributions to community projects)
- [ ] API strategy (public API availability, API-first design, developer ecosystem)
- [ ] Proprietary data assets (unique datasets, data network effects)
- [ ] AI and machine learning capabilities (models, applications, data science team)
- [ ] Security certifications (SOC 2, ISO 27001, FedRAMP, HIPAA)
- [ ] Compliance certifications (GDPR, CCPA, industry-specific)
- [ ] Technology partnerships (cloud providers, system integrators, technology alliances)
- [ ] Technical debt indicators (legacy systems, migration efforts, modernization initiatives)

## 8. Strategic Signals & Recent Activity (12 items)

- [ ] M&A activity in the last 24 months (acquisitions made, companies acquired by)
- [ ] Partnership announcements in the last 12 months
- [ ] Major product launches or updates in the last 12 months
- [ ] Geographic expansion moves (new offices, new market entries)
- [ ] Key executive hires in the last 12 months (name, role, prior company)
- [ ] Job posting patterns (which roles, which locations, volume trends)
- [ ] Patent filing patterns (new areas of invention, filing velocity)
- [ ] Press release themes and frequency
- [ ] Earnings call themes and management commentary (public companies)
- [ ] Investor day or analyst day takeaways (public companies)
- [ ] Regulatory or legal developments (lawsuits filed or received, regulatory approvals)
- [ ] Competitive response patterns (how they historically react to competitor moves)

---

## Data Collection Priority Guide

**Tier 1 - Must Have (collect for every competitor):**
Revenue, employee count, product portfolio, pricing, target market, key differentiators, recent strategic moves, top strengths and weaknesses.

**Tier 2 - Important (collect for top 3-5 competitors):**
Financial trends, leadership team, go-to-market model, technology stack, customer logos, partnership ecosystem, job posting analysis.

**Tier 3 - Nice to Have (collect opportunistically):**
Board composition, patent details, ESG initiatives, compensation data, detailed culture indicators, community metrics.

## Data Sources Quick Reference

| Data Category | Primary Sources |
|---------------|----------------|
| Financials | SEC filings, Crunchbase, PitchBook, annual reports |
| Products | Company website, G2, Capterra, product documentation |
| Employees | LinkedIn, Glassdoor, job boards |
| Technology | BuiltWith, StackShare, GitHub, patent databases |
| Market | Industry reports (Gartner, Forrester, IDC), press releases |
| Traffic | SimilarWeb, SEMrush, Ahrefs |
| Funding | Crunchbase, PitchBook, press releases |
| Strategy | Earnings calls, executive interviews, conference presentations |
