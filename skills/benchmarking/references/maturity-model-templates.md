# Maturity Model Templates

Ready-to-customize maturity assessment models for five core business functions. Each model uses a consistent 5-level scale from Ad Hoc (Level 1) to Optimized (Level 5). Adapt dimension descriptions to the specific organization and industry context.

---

## 1. Digital Maturity Model

Assess an organization's overall digital transformation progress across six dimensions.

| Dimension | Level 1: Ad Hoc | Level 2: Developing | Level 3: Defined | Level 4: Managed | Level 5: Optimized |
|-----------|----------------|-------------------|-----------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **Digital Strategy** | No digital strategy exists. Digital initiatives are reactive and uncoordinated. | Basic digital roadmap drafted but not linked to business strategy. Limited executive sponsorship. | Formal digital strategy aligned with business goals. Executive sponsor and governance in place. Funded portfolio of initiatives. | Digital embedded in enterprise strategy. Clear KPIs tracked quarterly. Portfolio actively managed with stage-gate reviews. | Digital is the business strategy. Continuous sensing of market shifts. Dynamic reallocation of digital investment based on real-time results. |
| **Customer Experience** | Customer interactions are channel-siloed. No unified view of the customer. | Some digital channels launched (website, basic app). Customer data exists in separate systems. | Omnichannel experience designed. Single customer ID across channels. Journey mapping completed for key segments. | Personalized experiences delivered using data. Real-time customer insights drive decisions. Seamless cross-channel handoffs. | Predictive and proactive engagement. AI-driven personalization at scale. Customer co-creation of products and services. Industry-leading NPS. |
| **Operations & Technology** | Legacy systems dominate. Manual processes and spreadsheets. Minimal integration between systems. | Some cloud adoption. Key processes partially digitized. Point-to-point integrations between critical systems. | Cloud-first policy. Core processes digitized end-to-end. API-based integration architecture. DevOps practices emerging. | Microservices architecture. Automated CI/CD pipelines. Real-time process monitoring. Scalable cloud infrastructure. | Fully composable architecture. Autonomous operations with AI-driven optimization. Zero-downtime deployments. Self-healing systems. |
| **Data & Analytics** | Data siloed in departments. Reporting is manual and retrospective. No data governance. | Central data warehouse established. Standard reports and dashboards available. Basic data quality rules defined. | Enterprise data platform with governed data lake. Self-service analytics available. Data literacy programs launched. | Advanced analytics (ML models) in production for key use cases. Data products managed as assets. Real-time data pipelines. | AI/ML embedded in core processes. Data monetization strategies active. Automated decision-making with human oversight. Organization-wide data fluency. |
| **Culture & Talent** | Resistance to change. Digital skills concentrated in IT. No digital training programs. | Awareness building underway. Pilot digital training programs. Innovation is everyone's job. External recognition as a digital leader. |
| **Innovation** | No structured innovation process. Ideas depend on individual initiative. | Innovation lab or sandbox created. Ideation sessions held periodically. Small experimentation budget. | Structured innovation pipeline with intake, evaluation, and funding stages. Measurable innovation ROI. | Ecosystem orchestrator. Platform-based innovation enabling partners and customers. Continuous disruption of own business model. Industry-shaping innovations. |

## Using These Templates

### Customization Guidelines
1. **Select relevant dimensions:** Not all six dimensions may apply equally. Add, remove, or rename dimensions to fit the organization's context.
2. **Refine level descriptions:** Adjust the language to reflect the organization's terminology, industry norms, and strategic priorities.
3. **Add evidence criteria:** For each cell, define 2-3 observable evidence items that confirm the organization is at that level.
4. **Calibrate with stakeholders:** Review and adjust the model with functional leaders before conducting assessments to ensure buy-in and relevance.

### Assessment Process
1. Distribute self-assessment surveys to functional leaders and their teams.
2. Conduct structured interviews to validate and challenge self-assessments with specific evidence.
3. Review documentation and data to independently verify claimed maturity levels.
4. Hold calibration sessions with cross-functional group to finalize scores.
5. Document evidence and rationale for each dimension score.
6. Compare against available external benchmarks (see benchmark-data-sources.md).
7. Identify priority gaps and develop an improvement roadmap with specific milestones for each level transition.
