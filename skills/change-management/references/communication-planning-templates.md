# Communication Planning Templates

## Audience Segmentation Matrix

Before crafting messages, segment your audiences by impact level and information needs:

| Audience Group | Impact Level | Information Needs | Preferred Channels | Frequency | Sender |
|---------------|:-----------:|-------------------|-------------------|-----------|--------|
| Executive team | Medium | Strategic rationale, business case, timeline, risks | 1:1 briefings, steering committee | Weekly | Change Lead |
| Middle managers | High | How to lead their teams through change, talking points, timeline | Manager briefings, toolkit, coaching | Bi-weekly | Change Lead + HR |
| Directly affected employees | Very High | What changes for me, timeline, training, support available | Town halls, team meetings, intranet, email | Weekly during transition | Direct manager + Change Lead |
| Indirectly affected employees | Low | Awareness of change, how it benefits the organization | All-hands, newsletter, intranet | Monthly | CEO/Executive Sponsor |
| External stakeholders | Varies | What changes for them, continuity assurance | Email, account manager calls, press release | As needed | Account team + Comms |

## Message Matrix Template

| Audience | Key Message | Supporting Points | Tone | Channel | Timing | Sender | Feedback Mechanism |
|----------|-----------|------------------|------|---------|--------|--------|--------------------|
| All employees | "We are [doing X] to [achieve Y]. Here's what it means for you." | 1. Why now 2. What changes 3. What stays the same 4. Support available | Transparent, confident | Town hall + email follow-up | Day 0 | CEO | Q&A at town hall, anonymous survey |
| Managers | "Your role is critical. Here's how to lead your team through this." | 1. Timeline 2. Talking points 3. FAQ 4. Escalation path | Empowering, practical | Manager briefing (1 day before all-hands) | Day -1 | Change Lead | Manager feedback channel |
| Affected team | "Here's specifically what changes for your role and how we'll support you." | 1. Role changes 2. Training plan 3. Timeline 4. Who to ask | Empathetic, specific | Team meeting + 1:1 follow-ups | Day 1-5 | Direct manager | 1:1 conversations, pulse survey |

## Channel Selection Guide

| Channel | Best For | Reach | Richness | Timing | Pros | Cons |
|---------|---------|:-----:|:--------:|--------|------|------|
| **CEO video/town hall** | Major announcements, vision-setting | High | High | Announcements | Personal, shows leadership commitment | One-way, hard to schedule |
| **All-hands meeting** | Updates, Q&A, celebration | High | High | Milestones | Interactive, builds community | Time-consuming, not everyone attends |
| **Manager cascade** | Detailed impact, team-level changes | Medium | Very High | Post-announcement | Trusted source, contextual, 2-way | Inconsistent delivery, manager skill varies |
| **Email** | Formal updates, documentation | High | Low | Regular updates | Permanent record, scalable | Easy to ignore, no emotion, overload |
| **Intranet/wiki** | Reference material, FAQ, resources | Medium | Medium | Ongoing | Self-service, always available | Low engagement, needs promotion |
| **Slack/Teams** | Quick updates, Q&A, community | Medium | Medium | Real-time | Fast, informal, interactive | Noisy, can spread rumors |
| **1:1 meetings** | Sensitive conversations, high-impact individuals | Low | Very High | As needed | Personalized, addresses individual concerns | Time-intensive, doesn't scale |
| **Newsletter** | Progress updates, success stories | High | Low-Medium | Weekly/bi-weekly | Consistent cadence, storytelling | Can feel impersonal |
| **Workshops** | Co-design, training, feedback | Low | Very High | Key phases | Deep engagement, builds ownership | Resource-intensive |
| **Posters/digital signage** | Awareness, reinforcement | High | Very Low | Ongoing | Ambient, low-effort | Can't convey complex messages |

## Communication Cadence by Phase

### Phase 1: Pre-Announcement (2–4 weeks before)

| Week | Activity | Audience | Channel | Owner |
|------|----------|----------|---------|-------|
| -4 | Brief executive team, align on messaging | Executives | 1:1 meetings | Change Lead |
| -3 | Brief board/advisory (if applicable) | Board | Board meeting | CEO |
| -2 | Brief middle managers, provide talking points | Managers | Manager briefing | Change Lead |
| -1 | Final message review, logistics prep | Change team | Working session | Comms Lead |
| -1 | Pre-brief key stakeholders (union, HR partners) | Key stakeholders | 1:1 | Change Lead |

### Phase 2: Announcement (Week 0)

| Day | Activity | Audience | Channel | Owner |
|-----|----------|----------|---------|-------|
| 0 (AM) | CEO announcement | All employees | Town hall / video | CEO |
| 0 (PM) | Email follow-up with details | All employees | Email | CEO |
| 0 (PM) | Manager Q&A session | Managers | Virtual meeting | Change Lead |
| 1–3 | Team meetings | Affected teams | Team meetings | Direct managers |
| 1–5 | 1:1 meetings with highly impacted individuals | Key individuals | 1:1 | Managers + HR |
| 3 | FAQ published | All employees | Intranet | Comms |
| 5 | External communications (if needed) | Customers, partners | Email, calls | Account team |

### Phase 3: Implementation (Ongoing)

| Frequency | Activity | Audience | Channel |
|-----------|----------|----------|---------|
| Weekly | Progress update email/newsletter | All employees | Email/newsletter |
| Bi-weekly | Manager check-in and coaching | Managers | Virtual meeting |
| Bi-weekly | Pulse survey (3–5 questions) | Affected employees | Survey tool |
| Monthly | Town hall update with Q&A | All employees | Town hall |
| Monthly | Success story spotlight | All employees | Newsletter, Slack |
| As needed | Issue response communications | Affected parties | Appropriate channel |

### Phase 4: Reinforcement (Post-transition)

| Frequency | Activity | Audience | Channel |
|-----------|----------|----------|---------|
| Monthly | Impact metrics update | All employees | Newsletter |
| Quarterly | "Look how far we've come" retrospective | All employees | Town hall |
| Ongoing | Recognition of adoption champions | Champions | All channels |
| 6-month | Comprehensive survey | All employees | Survey |

## Sample Communications

### 1. CEO Announcement Email

**Subject:** An Important Step Forward for [Company Name]

Team,

I'm writing to share an important change that will [state the outcome, not the activity — e.g., "position us to serve our customers better" not "restructure the organization"].

**Why now:** [2–3 sentences on the business context driving the change]

**What's changing:** [Clear, specific description of what changes]

**What's NOT changing:** [Reassure about what stays the same — values, mission, jobs (if true)]

**What this means for you:** [Honest summary of impact]

**How we'll support you:** [Training, resources, timeline]

**What happens next:**
- [Date]: Manager team meetings to discuss details
- [Date]: Training sessions begin
- [Date]: New [process/structure/system] goes live

I know change can be unsettling. I want you to hear directly from me that [reassurance about commitment to employees]. Your [manager/HR partner/change lead] is available to answer your questions.

I'll be hosting a town hall on [date] where I'll share more details and answer your questions directly.

[CEO Name]

### 2. Manager Talking Points

**For: [Change initiative name]**
**Date:** [Date]
**Audience:** Your direct reports

**Key messages to convey:**
1. [What is changing and why — in plain language]
2. [What it means specifically for this team]
3. [Timeline and key milestones]
4. [Support available (training, resources, contacts)]
5. [What you need from them]

**Anticipate these questions:**
- Q: "Will there be layoffs?" → A: [Honest answer]
- Q: "Why wasn't I consulted?" → A: [Acknowledge, explain process]
- Q: "What if the new system doesn't work?" → A: [Contingency plans]
- Q: "How will this affect my role/career?" → A: [Be specific per individual]

**Do's:**
- Be honest about what you know and don't know
- Acknowledge emotions — "I understand this is a lot to take in"
- Share your own perspective — "Here's why I support this..."
- Follow up individually with anyone who seems particularly concerned

**Don'ts:**
- Don't speculate or make promises you can't keep
- Don't minimize concerns — "It's not a big deal"
- Don't blame leadership — "They decided..."
- Don't rush the conversation — allow silence and questions

**Escalation:** If you encounter questions you can't answer, say "That's a great question. Let me find out and get back to you by [specific date]." Then contact [Change Lead] at [email].

### 3. FAQ Document

**[Initiative Name] — Frequently Asked Questions**
*Last updated: [Date]*

**General**
- **Q: What is changing?** A: [Clear, concise answer]
- **Q: Why is this change happening?** A: [Business rationale in plain language]
- **Q: When will this happen?** A: [Timeline with key dates]
- **Q: Who decided this?** A: [Decision process, who was involved]

**Impact on Employees**
- **Q: Will anyone lose their job?** A: [Direct, honest answer]
- **Q: Will my role change?** A: [Answer with specifics or timeline for specifics]
- **Q: Will my compensation change?** A: [Clear answer]
- **Q: Who will I report to?** A: [Answer or "finalized by X date"]

**Support & Training**
- **Q: What training will I receive?** A: [Training plan overview]
- **Q: Who can I talk to if I have concerns?** A: [Names, emails, anonymous channel]
- **Q: What if I'm struggling with the transition?** A: [Resources available]

**Timeline**
- **Q: What happens next?** A: [Next 3 milestones with dates]
- **Q: When will the new [system/structure] go live?** A: [Date]

*Have a question not answered here? Email [change@company.com] or ask in #change-initiative Slack channel.*

## Feedback Loop Design

### Channels for Capturing Sentiment

| Channel | Frequency | Format | Response Time | Owner |
|---------|-----------|--------|--------------|-------|
| Pulse surveys | Bi-weekly | 3–5 Likert scale + 1 open-ended | Summary within 48 hours | Change Lead |
| Town hall Q&A | Monthly | Live questions (verbal + digital) | Real-time + follow-up email | CEO + Change Lead |
| Manager feedback | Bi-weekly | Structured check-in template | Discussed at manager sync | Change Lead |
| Anonymous channel | Always open | Free-form submission | Addressed in weekly newsletter | Comms |
| 1:1 conversations | Ongoing | Unstructured | Immediate | Managers |
| Skip-level meetings | Monthly | Semi-structured | Within 1 week | Executives |

### Pulse Survey Template (5 Questions)

1. "I understand why this change is happening." (Strongly Disagree → Strongly Agree)
2. "I have the information I need about how this change affects me." (Strongly Disagree → Strongly Agree)
3. "I feel supported during this transition." (Strongly Disagree → Strongly Agree)
4. "I am confident this change will be successful." (Strongly Disagree → Strongly Agree)
5. "What is one thing we could do better to support you through this change?" (Open text)

### Acting on Feedback — The "Close the Loop" Process

1. **Collect** — Gather feedback through all channels
2. **Analyze** — Theme and quantify; identify top 3 issues
3. **Act** — Address what you can; acknowledge what you can't
4. **Communicate** — "You said X, we did Y" in the next update
5. **Track** — Monitor whether the issue improves in subsequent surveys

**Rule:** Never collect feedback you won't act on. Every survey result must lead to at least one visible action within 2 weeks.
