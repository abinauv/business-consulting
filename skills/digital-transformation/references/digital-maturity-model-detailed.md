# Digital Maturity Model — Detailed Assessment

## 8-Dimension Model

### Dimension 1: Strategy & Vision

| Level | Description | Indicators |
|:-----:|-----------|-----------|
| 1 — Ad Hoc | No digital strategy, IT seen as cost center | No digital budget, no executive ownership, reactive technology decisions |
| 2 — Emerging | Digital mentioned in strategy but not central | Some digital initiatives, no unified roadmap, siloed projects |
| 3 — Defined | Clear digital strategy aligned to business strategy | Digital roadmap exists, KPIs defined, dedicated budget, digital leadership role |
| 4 — Managed | Digital integral to business strategy, actively managed | Digital P&L tracking, portfolio management, regular strategy reviews |
| 5 — Optimized | Digital IS the strategy, continuous reinvention | Digital-first culture, disruption-aware, platform business model |

### Dimension 2: Customer Experience

| Level | Description | Indicators |
|:-----:|-----------|-----------|
| 1 | Minimal digital customer touchpoints | Static website, no online transactions, phone/email only |
| 2 | Basic digital presence | E-commerce or digital service, basic mobile, limited personalization |
| 3 | Multi-channel customer experience | Consistent experience across channels, some personalization, self-service |
| 4 | Omnichannel, data-driven CX | Unified customer view, predictive engagement, real-time personalization |
| 5 | Anticipatory, AI-powered CX | AI-driven next-best-action, proactive service, seamless phygital experience |

### Dimension 3: Operations & Processes

| Level | Description | Indicators |
|:-----:|-----------|-----------|
| 1 | Manual, paper-based processes | Spreadsheets, email-based workflows, no automation |
| 2 | Some digitization | Key processes in systems, but islands of automation |
| 3 | Standardized digital processes | Core processes in ERP/workflow tools, some RPA, KPI dashboards |
| 4 | Intelligent automation | End-to-end process automation, AI-augmented decisions, real-time monitoring |
| 5 | Autonomous operations | Self-optimizing processes, predictive operations, digital twins |

### Dimension 4: Technology & Architecture

| Level | Description | Indicators |
|:-----:|-----------|-----------|
| 1 | Legacy monolithic systems | On-premise only, custom-built, high technical debt, no APIs |
| 2 | Mixed legacy and modern | Some cloud, some APIs, but tightly coupled systems |
| 3 | Modern architecture emerging | Cloud migration underway, API-first for new builds, microservices adoption |
| 4 | Cloud-native, API-driven | Majority cloud, API ecosystem, CI/CD, DevOps culture |
| 5 | Composable, event-driven | Fully cloud-native, real-time event architecture, platform approach |

### Dimension 5: Data & Analytics

| Level | Description | Indicators |
|:-----:|-----------|-----------|
| 1 | Data in silos, no governance | Spreadsheet analytics, no single source of truth, data quality unknown |
| 2 | Basic reporting | Some dashboards, periodic reporting, limited data sharing |
| 3 | Advanced analytics | Data warehouse, self-service BI, data governance program, data quality monitoring |
| 4 | Predictive analytics | ML models in production, customer-level predictions, data-driven decisions |
| 5 | AI-native, data as asset | Real-time AI, data monetization, organization-wide data literacy, data mesh |

### Dimension 6: Organization & Culture

| Level | Description | Indicators |
|:-----:|-----------|-----------|
| 1 | Digital is IT's job | No digital skills outside IT, resistance to change, waterfall only |
| 2 | Digital awareness | Some digital training, digital champions emerging, limited agile |
| 3 | Digital competency building | Digital skills training program, agile teams, cross-functional collaboration |
| 4 | Digital-native mindset | Product thinking, design thinking, data literacy widespread, agile at scale |
| 5 | Continuous reinvention culture | Innovation labs, fail-fast culture, digital talent magnetism, learning organization |

### Dimension 7: Innovation & Agility

| Level | Description | Indicators |
|:-----:|-----------|-----------|
| 1 | No innovation process | No experimentation, risk-averse, long development cycles |
| 2 | Ad hoc innovation | Some experiments but no structure, innovation dependent on individuals |
| 3 | Structured innovation | Stage-gate for digital initiatives, innovation budget, hackathons |
| 4 | Systematic innovation | Innovation portfolio management, rapid prototyping, experiment culture |
| 5 | Innovation at scale | Continuous experimentation (A/B testing), innovation ecosystem, venture building |

### Dimension 8: Ecosystem & Partnerships

| Level | Description | Indicators |
|:-----:|-----------|-----------|
| 1 | Closed, internal focus | No technology partnerships, vendor management only, no API strategy |
| 2 | Select partnerships | Some technology vendors, basic integrations |
| 3 | Growing ecosystem | Partner program, marketplace or integrations, some co-creation |
| 4 | Open platform | API economy, developer ecosystem, strategic technology partnerships |
| 5 | Ecosystem orchestrator | Platform business, data sharing, co-innovation, ecosystem value creation |

## Self-Assessment Questionnaire

### Strategy & Vision (5 questions)
1. Does your organization have a formal digital strategy aligned to business strategy? (1: No / 3: Yes, basic / 5: Yes, comprehensive and regularly updated)
2. Is there a dedicated senior leader (CDO, CTO, or equivalent) accountable for digital? (1: No / 3: Shared responsibility / 5: Yes, with P&L authority)
3. What % of strategic initiatives have a digital component? (1: <10% / 3: 30–60% / 5: >80%)
4. Is there a dedicated digital transformation budget? (1: No / 3: Within IT budget / 5: Separate, ring-fenced budget)
5. How often is the digital strategy reviewed and updated? (1: Never / 3: Annually / 5: Quarterly with agile adaptation)

### Customer Experience (5 questions)
1. Can customers complete their primary journey entirely online/digitally? (1: No / 3: Partially / 5: Fully, with seamless experience)
2. Is customer data unified across all touchpoints? (1: No / 3: Partially / 5: Single customer view, real-time)
3. Do you use personalization in customer interactions? (1: No / 3: Basic segmentation / 5: AI-driven, real-time personalization)
4. What is your Net Promoter Score for digital channels? (1: Below 0 / 3: 20–40 / 5: >50)
5. Can customers seamlessly switch between channels? (1: Channels are separate / 3: Some integration / 5: Fully omnichannel)

### Operations & Processes (5 questions)
1. What % of core business processes are digitized end-to-end? (1: <20% / 3: 40–60% / 5: >80%)
2. Do you use automation (RPA, workflow) for repetitive tasks? (1: No / 3: Some pilots / 5: At scale, >20 processes)
3. Are operational KPIs available in real-time dashboards? (1: Monthly reports / 3: Weekly dashboards / 5: Real-time, automated)
4. What is your straight-through processing rate for key transactions? (1: <30% / 3: 50–70% / 5: >90%)
5. Do you use predictive analytics for operations (demand forecasting, maintenance, quality)? (1: No / 3: Pilots / 5: Production, multiple use cases)

## Common Maturity Patterns

| Pattern | Profile | Diagnosis | Recommendation |
|---------|---------|-----------|---------------|
| **"All talk, no tech"** | High strategy, low technology/data | Strategy exists but can't execute — infrastructure gap | Invest in technology foundation before more strategy |
| **"Islands of excellence"** | 1–2 dimensions advanced, rest lagging | Digital pockets exist but aren't scaled | Create governance to spread best practices, unify architecture |
| **"Tech-forward, culture-behind"** | High technology, low culture/organization | Built the tools but nobody uses them | Invest in change management, training, incentive alignment |
| **"Data-rich, insight-poor"** | Moderate data capability, low analytics | Collecting data but not creating value from it | Hire analytics talent, invest in BI tools, create data culture |
| **"Customer-obsessed, operations-lagging"** | High CX, low operations | Great front-end but broken back-end | Invest in operational automation, integrate front-to-back |
