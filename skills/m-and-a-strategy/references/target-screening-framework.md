# Target Screening Framework

## Strategic Rationale Articulation

Before screening targets, articulate WHY you're pursuing M&A:

### M&A Thesis Templates

| Strategy | Thesis Template | Example |
|----------|---------------|---------|
| **Scale** | "Acquire to achieve scale in [market] to [reduce costs / increase market power / improve margins]" | "Acquire regional competitor to become #1 in Northeast US, achieving procurement leverage and branch network density" |
| **Scope** | "Acquire to expand into [new market/product/geography] that is [adjacent/complementary] to our core" | "Acquire SaaS analytics platform to add recurring revenue to our services business" |
| **Capability** | "Acquire to obtain [technology/talent/process] that would take [X years] to build organically" | "Acquire AI/ML team to accelerate product development by 2 years vs. internal build" |
| **Transformation** | "Acquire to fundamentally transform our business model from [current] to [future]" | "Acquire D2C brand to shift from wholesale-dependent to omnichannel" |
| **Consolidation** | "Acquire to consolidate fragmented industry, improve margins through scale, and create platform for add-ons" | "Acquire 3–5 regional HVAC companies to build national platform with 15%+ EBITDA margin" |

### Buy vs. Build vs. Partner Decision Framework

| Factor | Build | Buy | Partner |
|--------|:-----:|:---:|:-------:|
| **Speed to market** | Slow (12–36 mo) | Fast (3–6 mo) | Medium (6–12 mo) |
| **Capital required** | Low-medium (spread over time) | High (upfront) | Low-medium |
| **Execution risk** | Medium (internal capability) | High (integration) | Medium (alignment) |
| **Control** | Full | Full (post-integration) | Shared |
| **Culture risk** | None | High | Medium |
| **Best when** | Core to strategy, time allows, talent available | Speed critical, capability unavailable, market consolidating | Non-core, testing market, shared risk desired |

**Decision rule:** Buy when (a) time-to-market advantage justifies premium, AND (b) integration risk is manageable, AND (c) the capability can't be replicated organically within the strategic window.

## Screening Criteria Development

### Must-Haves vs. Nice-to-Haves

| Category | Must-Haves (deal-breakers if not met) | Nice-to-Haves (add points if present) |
|----------|--------------------------------------|---------------------------------------|
| **Strategic** | In target market/segment, no regulatory blocking issues | Market leader or #2, strong brand, IP portfolio |
| **Financial** | Revenue >$X, positive EBITDA (or path to it), clean financials | Revenue growing >15%, EBITDA margin >20%, low capex |
| **Operational** | Proven product/service, established customer base | Scalable platform, strong management team, efficient operations |
| **Cultural** | No ethical/legal red flags, compatible values | Entrepreneurial culture, strong employee engagement |
| **Geographic** | Presence in target markets | Expansion potential, low geographic overlap |

### Scoring Model Template

| Criteria | Weight | 1 (Poor) | 3 (Average) | 5 (Excellent) |
|----------|:------:|----------|-------------|----------------|
| **Strategic fit** | 25% | Tangential to strategy | Aligned but not critical | Core to strategy, fills key gap |
| **Revenue quality** | 15% | Declining, concentrated | Stable, moderate concentration | Growing, diversified, recurring |
| **Profitability** | 15% | Negative EBITDA | 5–15% margin | >20% margin, expanding |
| **Growth potential** | 10% | Flat or declining market | Moderate growth | High-growth market, strong pipeline |
| **Management team** | 10% | Key person risk, weak bench | Adequate | Strong, deep bench, stays post-close |
| **Integration complexity** | 10% | Major integration challenges | Moderate, manageable | Easy to integrate, complementary systems |
| **Cultural fit** | 5% | Significant cultural clash risk | Neutral | Highly compatible culture |
| **Customer overlap** | 5% | High overlap (>50%) | Moderate (20–50%) | Complementary (<20% overlap) |
| **Price expectation** | 5% | Unrealistic expectations | Market multiples | Below market, motivated seller |

## Screening Funnel

```
Universe (100+ companies)
    │  Apply must-have criteria (automatic filters)
    ▼
Long List (20-30 companies)
    │  Desktop research, preliminary scoring
    ▼
Short List (5-10 companies)
    │  Management meetings, preliminary DD, indicative valuation
    ▼
LOI Targets (1-3 companies)
    │  Full due diligence, negotiation
    ▼
Signed Deal (1 company)
    │  Regulatory approval, close
    ▼
Integration
```

### Stage Gate Criteria

| Gate | From → To | Criteria to Pass |
|------|----------|-----------------|
| 1 | Universe → Long List | Meets all must-haves, no obvious red flags |
| 2 | Long List → Short List | Scoring model >3.5/5, strategic fit confirmed, preliminary valuation reasonable |
| 3 | Short List → LOI | Management meeting positive, key DD items validated, valuation range aligned |
| 4 | LOI → Close | Full DD passed, definitive agreement terms acceptable, financing secured |

## Target Profile Template

For each short-listed target, compile:

| Section | Data Points |
|---------|------------|
| **Company overview** | Name, HQ, founding year, ownership, employee count, description |
| **Products/services** | Key offerings, differentiation, technology, IP |
| **Financials** | Revenue (3-year trend), EBITDA, margins, revenue mix, customer concentration |
| **Market position** | Market share, competitive positioning, brand strength |
| **Customers** | Top customers, customer count, retention rate, contract structure |
| **Management** | Key leaders, tenure, depth of bench, likelihood to stay post-close |
| **Growth** | Revenue growth rate, pipeline, expansion opportunities |
| **Strategic fit** | Synergy potential, capability gaps filled, market access gained |
| **Risks** | Key risks, regulatory, customer concentration, technology, legal |
| **Valuation** | Comparable multiples, implied range, seller expectations |
| **Integration** | Complexity assessment, critical dependencies, timeline |
| **Recommendation** | Pursue / Monitor / Pass (with rationale) |

## Red Flags Checklist

Stop or proceed with extreme caution if you find:

| Category | Red Flag | Why It Matters |
|----------|---------|---------------|
| **Financial** | Revenue declining >10% YoY without clear fix | You're buying a melting ice cube |
| **Financial** | Customer concentration >30% one customer | One lost customer could destroy the business case |
| **Financial** | Aggressive revenue recognition | Financials may not be what they seem |
| **Legal** | Pending material litigation | Unknown liability, management distraction |
| **Regulatory** | Change-of-control triggers in key contracts/licenses | Deal could cause loss of critical assets |
| **People** | Key person risk (1–2 people are the business) | If they leave, you've bought nothing |
| **People** | Management won't commit to stay | Transition risk dramatically increases |
| **Technology** | Technical debt >2 years to remediate | Hidden costs, integration timeline explodes |
| **Culture** | History of failed integrations or toxic culture reports | Integration risk is very high |
| **Market** | Market is in structural decline | Growth thesis is fundamentally flawed |
| **Competitive** | Losing share rapidly to well-funded competitors | May be too late to catch up |
| **Ethics** | Environmental, safety, or compliance violations | Reputation risk, potential fines |

## Worked Example: Technology Platform Acquisition Screening

**Context:** $200M revenue enterprise software company seeking to acquire a data analytics platform to enhance its product suite.

### Screening Criteria

**Must-haves:** Data analytics/BI platform, $5M+ ARR, positive unit economics, B2B enterprise focus, compatible tech stack (cloud-native), no regulatory blocking issues

**Nice-to-haves:** >30% YoY growth, net revenue retention >110%, established enterprise customer base, team willing to stay, AI/ML capabilities

### Scoring Results

| Target | Strategic Fit (25%) | Revenue Quality (15%) | Profit (15%) | Growth (10%) | Team (10%) | Integration (10%) | Culture (5%) | Customer (5%) | Price (5%) | **Total** |
|--------|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **DataCo** | 5 | 4 | 3 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 3 | **4.15** |
| **AnalytiX** | 4 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 4 | **3.75** |
| **InsightAI** | 5 | 3 | 2 | 5 | 5 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 2 | **3.65** |
| **ChartHub** | 3 | 4 | 5 | 2 | 3 | 5 | 3 | 2 | 5 | **3.55** |
| **VizPlatform** | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 2 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 4 | **2.85** |

**Recommendation:**
1. **DataCo** — Top scorer, best strategic fit, high growth, complementary customers. Proceed to management meeting and preliminary DD.
2. **AnalytiX** — Strong revenue quality and profitability, good price. Proceed as backup option.
3. **InsightAI** — Excellent team and technology (AI/ML), but pre-profit and expensive. Monitor; revisit if they raise a bridge round.
4. **ChartHub** — Profitable but low growth, low strategic value. Pass.
5. **VizPlatform** — Below threshold on most criteria. Pass.
